## Learnings & Notes

### What Works
- Professional, authoritative tone
- Bullet point lists for easy scanning
- Questions as hooks ("What are buyers looking for?")
- Data and statistics when available (28 years experience, 250+ sold)
- Client testimonials and success stories
- Educational content that positions CWSA as industry experts
- Linking to blog articles for deeper content
- Short paragraphs with clear structure
- Emojis used sparingly for bullet points (📊 📍 🔧 📈 ✅)

### What to Avoid
- Overly promotional language
- Promises or guarantees about returns
- Casual/informal tone
- Excessive emojis
- Generic business advice (keep car wash industry-specific)
- Salesy language - they position as advisors, not salespeople
- Using "we" too much - focus on client benefits

### Caption Style Observations
- Start with a question or direct statement
- Use line breaks between paragraphs for readability
- Bullet points with emojis work well for lists
- Keep hashtags to 3-6 per post
- Always include #CWSA as primary hashtag
- End with soft CTA ("Learn more", "Let's discuss") not hard sell
