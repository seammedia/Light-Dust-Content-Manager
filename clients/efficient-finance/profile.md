## LEARNINGS & AI IMAGE GUIDELINES

### Critical Rules for Image Generation
1. **THIS IS A FINANCE BROKER** - They help people get loans for assets. They are NOT:
   - An electrical company
   - A trades business
   - A service provider

2. **Images should show THE ASSET being financed:**
   - Boats on water, at marina, being enjoyed
   - Cars (new, shiny, aspirational)
   - Caravans at campgrounds, on the road
   - Jet skis on the water
   - Trucks and utes (clean, professional)
   - Motorhomes parked at scenic locations

3. **DO NOT include in images:**
   - Logos or text (we add branding separately if needed)
   - Workers, tradespeople, hi-vis vests
   - Electrical equipment, switchboards, tools
   - Office/finance paperwork scenes
   - Tablets or screens showing "finance apps"

4. **Image prompt style:**
   - "Professional stock photo of [asset] in Australian setting"
   - "Aspirational lifestyle photo of [asset]"
   - "Beautiful [asset] photograph, sunny day, no people or minimal people enjoying"

### What Went Wrong (Jan 2026)
- AI confused "Equipment Finance for Business" with electrical services
- Generated images of electrical vans, switchboards, workers in hi-vis
- Tried to add fake "Efficient Finance" branding to vehicles
- Mixed up context from other clients (Pace Electrical)

### How to Fix
- Be VERY explicit in image prompts that this is a FINANCE company
- Focus prompts on the ASSET (the boat, car, caravan) not the business
- Never mention "services" or "business operations" in image prompts
- Use prompts like: "Stock photo of luxury boat on calm water, Australian coast, aspirational lifestyle photography"
