- Headings written as plain text (apply H1/H2/H3 formatting in WordPress)
- Bullet points written as plain text lines (apply bullet formatting in WordPress)

### SEO Checklist (must pass before submitting)
- Focus keyword appears EXACTLY in SEO title
- Focus keyword appears EXACTLY in meta description
- Focus keyword appears EXACTLY in URL/slug
- Focus keyword appears in the first paragraph (beginning of content)
- Focus keyword appears in at least one H2 subheading

- Content is 600+ words
- Include at least one external link to authoritative source
- Include at least one internal link to existing blog post
