## CRITICAL LEARNINGS (From Client Feedback)

### Image-Caption Matching Rules

**IMPORTANT: The image MUST match the caption content!**

| If caption mentions... | Image MUST show... |
|------------------------|-------------------|
| Dog wash, pup, pooch | Dogs being washed, dog wash station |
| Car wash, touch-free | Automated car wash bay, NOT hand washing |
| Family activity | Family-friendly scene matching the service |

### DO NOT USE These Image Types

1. **Hand washing images** - Washco is a TOUCH-FREE automated wash
   - ❌ People with sponges/cloths washing cars
   - ❌ Hand detailing images
   - ✅ Automated wash bays, foam jets, touchless equipment

2. **Mismatched service images**
   - ❌ Dog wash image with car wash caption
   - ❌ Car wash image with dog wash caption
   - ✅ Always match image to the service being promoted

### Client Feedback Examples

**Feedback 1:** "The text doesn't match the image - either update image or update text to talk to car washing please."
- **Issue:** Caption about dog washing, but image showed car wash
- **Fix:** Either change image to dog wash OR change caption to car wash

**Feedback 2:** "Please update image - the text talks to 'touch free' yet the image shows a hand wash. REMINDER please don't use hand washing images."
- **Issue:** Caption mentioned "touch-free wash" but image showed someone hand washing a car
- **Fix:** Use automated/touchless car wash images only

**Feedback 3:** "Please choose another image that speaks to the text - not hand washing"
- **Issue:** Same as above - hand washing image used
- **Fix:** Source or generate images of automated wash bays

### Touch-Free Messaging

When writing about the car wash:
- Emphasize "touch-free" technology
- No brushes, no contact, no scratches
- Protects paint finish
- Modern, automated equipment
- "Don't lift a finger" messaging

**Approved image types for touch-free posts:**
- Automated wash bay with foam/water jets
- Car inside automated tunnel
- Touchless equipment close-ups
- Shiny finished car (result shot)
- Exterior of wash facility

**NOT approved:**
- Hand washing with sponge/mitt
- Bucket and hose washing
- Manual detailing
- Any human physically touching/washing car

## COMMON MISTAKES TO AVOID

1. **Using stock "car wash" images that show hand washing**
   - Many stock photos show manual/hand washing
   - Always verify image shows AUTOMATED/TOUCHLESS wash

2. **Mixing up dog wash and car wash content**
   - Double-check image matches the caption topic
   - Don't assume - verify before publishing

3. **Generic car cleaning images**
   - Avoid: someone wiping down a car
   - Avoid: bucket and sponge setups
   - Use: foam cannons, water jets, automated bays

4. **Forgetting the touch-free USP**
   - This is their key differentiator
   - Always reinforce automated/touchless when relevant
