## LEARNINGS & NOTES

### Client History
- **Status:** First month client (January 2026)
- **Feedback received:** Client felt initial content was too close to website copy
- **Resolution:** Rewrote all captions with stronger hooks and trending formats

### CRITICAL RULES
- **NEVER use em dashes (—) in captions** - Use regular dashes (-) or rewrite sentences instead
- **NEVER use em dashes in emails** - Keep all communications em dash-free
- **NEVER use hashtags in captions** - Hashtags go in the separate hashtags field only, NOT in caption text
- **NEVER use hashtags in emails** - Keep all client communications hashtag-free
- **DO NOT copy website language** - Create original, engaging content that differs from product descriptions
- **DO NOT offer phone calls** - Client prefers written communication only

### Image Guidelines
- DO NOT use AI-generated images
- Download actual product photos from their Shopify CDN
- Use lifestyle shots showing kids wearing the clothes
- Beach, backyard, outdoor settings preferred

### Caption Guidelines (Updated January 2026)
- **USE TRENDING HOOKS** -Client specifically requested stronger hooks and trending topics
- Avoid "Meet the [Product Name]" format (too close to website copy)
- Use relatable parent pain points as hooks
- Trending formats that work:
  - "POV: [relatable parent moment]"
  - "Unpopular opinion: [controversial-ish take]"
  - "Tell me you have a [type of kid] without telling me..."
  - Before/after scenarios
  - "Things I wish I knew before..."
- Include social proof cues ("limited edition", "selling fast", "most requested")
- Add clear CTAs ("Shop via link in bio", "Tap to shop")
- Focus on how it FEELS (buttery-soft, like a hug, like sunshine)
- Address parent concerns (durability, washability)
- Keep it playful and warm
- **NO HASHTAGS in captions**
