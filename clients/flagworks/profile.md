## Brand Identity

### Voice & Tone
- Patriotic, proud
- Quality-focused
- Australian through and through
- Professional but approachable
- Trustworthy, reliable

### Key Messages
- Premium quality, fully sewn flags (not printed)
- Handcrafted with exceptional attention to detail
- Made the traditional way
- UV-treated for Australian conditions
- Built to last outdoors
- Free Australia-wide shipping
- Fast dispatch, exceptional service

### Words to Use
- Fully sewn, hand-crafted, premium
- UV-treated, durable, weatherproof
- Appliqued stars, double stitching, reinforced
- Fly it proud, Aussie pride
- Traditional craftsmanship
- Quality you can see

### Words to Avoid
- Cheap
- Printed (their flags are sewn, not printed)
- Basic
