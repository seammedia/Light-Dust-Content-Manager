## Brand Identity

### Voice & Tone
- Warm, approachable, and client-focused
- Professional yet compassionate
- Emphasizes support and guidance during difficult times
- Client-centric messaging - "It's never about us. It's your needs that matter most."

### Key Messages
- "Advice | Guidance | Support"
- "We are not just lawyers. We advise, lead, guide and support you. We walk with you on your journey. We have your back."
- "Actually... It's All About You!"
- "Be Supported. Be Guided. Be Assured."
- "Needing Quick, Friendly, Easy Advice"

### Words to Use
- Advice, Support, Guidance
- Understanding, Compassion, Empathy
- Experience, Expertise
- Personalised, Client-first
- Straightforward, Transparent

### Words to Avoid
- Overly technical legal jargon
- Cold or impersonal language
- Aggressive litigation terminology
